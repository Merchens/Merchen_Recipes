## Ingredients

- 2 1/4 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 cup butter, softened
- 3/4 cup sugar
- 3/4 cup packed brown sugar
- 2 eggs
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 2 cups chocolate chips

## Instructions

1. Heat oven to 375°F.
2. Whisk flour, baking soda and salt in a small bowl.
3. Beat butter and both sugars until creamy, then beat in eggs and vanilla.
4. Gradually mix in the flour mixture, then stir in the chocolate chips.
5. Drop rounded tablespoons onto ungreased baking sheets.
6. Bake 9–11 minutes until golden. Cool on the sheet for 2 minutes.
